Ask my portfolio · in-browser RAG

Ask a question. Get an answer with sources.

This is a small retrieval system built from what's on this page. It ranks 25 passages with BM25, writes the answer from the best matches, and links every claim to its source. When nothing matches, it says so instead of guessing.

tokenizeLowercase, drop stop words, light stemming, synonyms
retrieveBM25 over 25 passages, top 3 above a relevance floor
composeGrounded sentences with numbered citations

It runs entirely in your browser. No LLM, no network calls, nothing leaves the page.
